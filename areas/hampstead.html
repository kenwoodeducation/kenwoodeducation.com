How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us about your child

    Send a message or fill in our short form. Year group, what they need help with, and any target grade or school.

  2. 2

    We recommend, you decide

    We match on subject, exam board, teaching style and personality, and share the profiles of the tutors who fit best. If one tutor clearly stands out, we’ll recommend just them.

    Your tutor gets in touch

    Your tutor contacts you the same day, by phone or email, to introduce themselves and arrange the first lesson. If you’re happy, you carry on directly with your tutor, with notes after every lesson. If it’s not the right fit, your first lesson with a new tutor is on us.
