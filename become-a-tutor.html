Why tutor with Kenwood

I started out as a full-time tutor, so I know what makes the work sustainable: fair pay, short journeys and families who stay. Kenwood is built around those things.

Ondi, Founder

Set your own rate

You decide your hourly rate, and that’s exactly what you receive. We add our margin on top when invoicing the family, and our margins are among the lowest of any tutoring agency or platform, in person or online. If you’d like a steer on where to pitch your rate, just ask.

Teach close to home

We match you with families near where you live, so you’re not crossing London between lessons. Shorter journeys mean more time teaching, and it’s easier to keep working with the same students for the long term.

We handle the admin

Matching, invoicing and payment collection all run through TutorCruncher, and your earnings are paid out to you monthly, so you never have to chase an invoice.

Work on your terms

You’re never obliged to accept a match, and we’re not obliged to offer a fixed amount of work. Many of our tutors also work with other agencies or private students.

What we look for

We’re selective about who joins us. Our tutors bring strong academic credentials (many from Oxbridge and Russell Group universities), real teaching experience, or both. Some are qualified teachers, heads of department or examiners. We also welcome current university students and newer tutors with an excellent academic record, vetted in exactly the same way.

Expert in your subject

Deep knowledge of the subjects and levels you teach, whatever they are, from primary to A‑Level and entrance exams.

Based locally

You live in or near Highgate, Hampstead, Muswell Hill, Crouch End or East Finchley, so you can get to families easily.

Genuinely engaging

Patient and encouraging, and able to adapt to how each student learns best, not just deep subject knowledge.

How to apply

  1. 1

    Email us your CV

    Tell us the subjects and levels you’d like to teach, and which part of London you live in, for example Highgate or Muswell Hill.

  2. 2

    Interview

    We’ll talk through your experience, your approach and your suitability to work with children, and how Kenwood works day to day.

  3. 3

    References and DBS

    You complete our onboarding form. We verify two references and your enhanced DBS check, or apply for one on your behalf.

  4. 4

    Matched with families

    We let you know when a family is a good fit. If they choose you, you get in touch the same day to arrange the first lesson.
