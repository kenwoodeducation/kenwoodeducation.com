Tutor on your own terms,
with families who value it

Kenwood Education introduces vetted tutors to families across North London. You set your own rate, choose the work you take on, and we handle the admin — matching, invoicing, and payment — so you can focus on teaching.

What we look for

We accept just 1 in 18 applicants. Every tutor on our roster is either Oxbridge or Russell Group educated with a strong academic track record, or brings genuine, extensive teaching experience — some as qualified teachers, heads of department, or examiners.

Strong academic credentials, or real experience

We're looking for tutors who are either Oxbridge or Russell Group educated, or who bring a genuine, proven track record of teaching or tutoring success.

Based locally

We only work with tutors local to the areas we serve — Highgate, Hampstead, Muswell Hill, Crouch End, East Finchley and surrounding North London.

Genuinely engaging with students

Patient, encouraging, and able to adapt to how a particular student learns best — not just deep subject knowledge, but the ability to bring it to life.

Why tutor with Kenwood

A straightforward, well-supported way to build a tutoring practice.

Set your own rate

You decide your hourly rate. We add a margin when invoicing the family — what you charge is exactly what you receive. Our Fee Guidance document offers suggested rates based on experience, but the final number is always your choice.

We handle the admin

Matching, invoicing, and payment collection all run through TutorCruncher. Funds split automatically the moment a family pays — no chasing invoices yourself.

Work on your terms

You're never obliged to accept a match, and we're not obliged to offer a fixed amount of work. Most of our tutors work with other agencies or private students alongside Kenwood.

A supported professional community

Kenwood is a Corporate Member of The Tutors' Association. We're responsive, and you're never navigating a placement alone.

How to apply

1

Email us your CV

Send your CV to hello@kenwoodeducation.com, along with the subjects and levels you'd like to teach.

2

Structured interview

We'll assess your experience, approach, and suitability to work with children, and talk through how Kenwood works day to day.

3

Full details, references and DBS check

If it's a good fit, we'll send you our onboarding form to complete. We'll verify both references, and confirm your enhanced DBS check — or apply on your behalf if you don't already hold one.

4

Matched with families

Once approved, we'll contact you directly whenever a family looks like a good fit for your subjects, level, and location.
