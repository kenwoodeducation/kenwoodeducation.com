I started out as a full-time tutor. I loved the work, but I saw its problems up close. Long commutes across London meant I sometimes had to stop working with students just as they were making real progress. As a business leader at global, publicly listed companies, I’ve spent many years leading teams and hiring top talent. That taught me how to spot exceptional people, and what excellent service should feel like.

Kenwood brings the two together. Our tutors live locally, so they can stay with your child for the long run. We’re open with our tutors about the margin we take, so the best ones want to work with us. And I choose every tutor to the same high standard I’ve always hired to.

Frequently asked questions

Why use an agency rather than finding a tutor yourself? +

A tutor found through a Facebook group or Nextdoor post hasn’t been checked by anyone. We interview every tutor, check their references and enhanced DBS, and choose the one who fits your child. If the first match isn’t right, we find you someone else.

What is your approach to safeguarding? +

An enhanced DBS check, the highest level of UK government background check, is required for every tutor before they meet any family. On top of that, every tutor agrees to our Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct, and for students under 18 a responsible adult must be at home throughout the lesson. If anything ever feels wrong, we want to hear about it straight away.

Do you only offer in-person tutoring? +

Yes. We believe a tutor in the same room gets better results for most children, especially those who need confidence as much as content. We focus on doing in-person well.

How does payment work? Is there anything to pay upfront? +

Nothing upfront and no registration fee. Your first lesson is half price, and you only add a card afterwards. Each lesson is then invoiced automatically once it’s taken place, and paid by card through Stripe. There’s no subscription or separate agency fee.

What if the tutor isn’t the right fit? +

That’s why your first lesson is 50% off. If it isn’t right, tell us and we’ll find someone better.

What if you don’t have a tutor that fits? +

We’d rather tell you than offer a partial match. We may offer a dedicated search for a new tutor, usually within two weeks, vetted in exactly the same way. A £100 deposit starts the search: it comes off your first invoice if we place a tutor, and is refunded in full if we can’t.
