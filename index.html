Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost, and is there anything to pay upfront?

Each tutor’s rate depends on their experience. See our pricing for typical rates. There’s no registration fee and nothing to pay upfront. Each lesson is invoiced once it’s taken place and paid by card through Stripe.

What’s your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or move a lesson. Lessons cancelled with less notice may be charged in full, as your tutor will have kept that time free for you.

How do you keep my child safe?

Every tutor has an interview, two references and an enhanced DBS check, the highest level of UK background check, before they meet any family. Every tutor also agrees to our Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct. If anything ever feels wrong, we want to hear about it straight away.

Where do lessons take place?

In your home. We specialise in in-person tutoring because a tutor in the same room gets better results for most children, especially those who need confidence as much as content. For students under 18, a responsible adult must be at home throughout the lesson.

What if the tutor isn’t the right fit?

Tell us within 24 hours of your first lesson and we’ll suggest other tutors for you to choose from. Your first lesson with the new tutor is on us. If things change further down the line, just let us know and we’ll suggest other tutors then too.

Why use an agency rather than finding a tutor yourself?

A tutor found through a Facebook group or Nextdoor post hasn’t been checked by anyone. We vet every tutor and recommend the ones who best fit your child. Your chosen tutor then gets in touch to introduce themselves before the first lesson.
