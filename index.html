What is a DBS check? A Disclosure and Barring Service check is an official UK government background check confirming a person has no criminal history that would make them unsuitable to work with children. We require an enhanced DBS check — the highest level — for every tutor before they meet any family.

Why in-person matters

Online tutoring became popular during the pandemic — but for most students, especially younger children and those who need confidence as much as content, a tutor sitting with your child in person is simply better.

A tutor can see when a child is lost

On a screen, it’s easy to nod along without understanding. In person, a good tutor picks up on body language, frustration, and confusion instantly — and adjusts in the moment.

Fewer distractions, better focus

Children learning at home online are surrounded by distractions. A tutor physically present creates a contained, focused environment that online sessions simply can’t replicate.

Relationships build confidence

Many children struggling academically are also struggling with confidence. The trust built through regular in-person sessions is harder to build through a screen — and often as important as the subject knowledge itself.
