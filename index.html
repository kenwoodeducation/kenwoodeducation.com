Why in-person matters

Online tutoring became popular during the pandemic — but for most students, especially younger children and those who need confidence as much as content, a tutor sitting with your child in person is simply better.

A tutor can see when a child is lost

On a screen, it’s easy to nod along without understanding. In person, a good tutor picks up on body language, frustration, and confusion instantly — and adjusts in the moment.

Fewer distractions, better focus

Children learning at home online are surrounded by distractions. A tutor physically present creates a contained, focused environment that online sessions simply can’t replicate.

Relationships build confidence

Many children struggling academically are also struggling with confidence. The trust built through regular in-person sessions is harder to build through a screen — and often as important as the subject knowledge itself.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a private tutor cost in North London? +

Private tutors in North London typically charge between £40 and £100+ per hour depending on experience and subject. Through Kenwood Education, pricing reflects the tutor’s experience and qualifications — from talented graduates through to specialist examiners and former teachers. We’ll discuss the right option during our initial conversation based on your child’s specific needs and your budget.

What is an enhanced DBS check? +

An enhanced DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) check is the highest level of UK government background check. It confirms a person has no criminal history that would make them unsuitable to work with children. We require an enhanced DBS check for every tutor before they meet any family - it is non-negotiable.

Why use a tutoring agency rather than finding a tutor independently? +

When you find a tutor through a Facebook group or Nextdoor post, you have no way of verifying their qualifications, checking their background, or knowing whether they’re the right fit for your child. A tutoring agency does all of that on your behalf - vetting every tutor, matching them carefully to your child, and remaining accountable if something isn’t working.

How do you match tutors to students? +

We don’t use an algorithm. When you contact us, we take time to understand your child - their year group, current level, target grade or school, learning style, and personality. We then identify the right tutor from our vetted pool based on subject specialism, exam board knowledge, teaching style, and location. Every match is personal.

Which subjects do you cover? +

We cover all core subjects from primary (KS2) through to A-level, as well as music lessons. Our most in-demand subjects are GCSE Maths, GCSE English, GCSE Sciences, 11+ preparation, and A-level Maths. If you need a subject not listed, get in touch and we’ll let you know whether we can help.

What areas do you cover? +

We primarily serve Highgate, Hampstead, Muswell Hill, Crouch End, and East Finchley. We also cover surrounding areas including Hornsey, Archway, Finsbury Park, Barnet, and Kentish Town. If you’re unsure whether we cover your area, get in touch and we’ll confirm.

What happens if the tutor isn’t the right fit? +

That’s exactly why we offer a half-price trial lesson. If after the first session you feel the match isn’t right, tell us and we’ll find someone better. We stay involved throughout every placement - if something isn’t working at any point, we want to know and we’ll fix it.

Do you only offer in-person tutoring? +

Yes - we specialise exclusively in in-person tutoring. We believe a tutor sitting with your child in the same room produces better outcomes than online sessions for most students, particularly those who need confidence-building as much as content. There are plenty of online tutoring platforms if that’s what you’re looking for - we focus on doing in-person well.
