Why Kenwood Education

We do the hard work so you don’t have to. Here’s what that looks like in practice.

Oxbridge & Russell Group-educated tutors

Most of our tutors are educated to a genuinely high standard, from Oxbridge and Russell Group universities. In exceptional cases, we also work with tutors who bring outstanding credentials instead, such as a long, proven track record of teaching or tutoring success, or experience as a head of department.

We vet so you don’t have to

We accept just 1 in 18 applicants. Our interview and vetting process takes up to four weeks, covering background checks, references and subject knowledge, alongside an enhanced DBS check. You get someone you can trust walking through your door.

Selectively matched to your child

Other agencies post jobs on a board and let tutors bid for them. We don’t. We take the time to understand your child’s goals, learning style and personality, then personally match them with a tutor we believe is genuinely the right fit.

What is a DBS check? A Disclosure and Barring Service check is an official UK government background check confirming a person has no criminal history that would make them unsuitable to work with children. We require an enhanced DBS check, the highest level, for every tutor before they meet any family.

Safeguarding is ongoing, not a one-off check. Every tutor agrees to our Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct before they’re introduced to a family, covering professional boundaries and how any concern is reported and handled. A responsible adult must be present in the property throughout every session involving a student under 18. If anything ever feels wrong to a family or a tutor, we want to hear about it immediately: we stay responsible for safeguarding throughout the placement, not just at the point of introduction.

Why in-person matters

Online tutoring became popular during the pandemic, but for most students, especially younger children and those who need confidence as much as content, a tutor sitting with your child in person is simply better.

A tutor can see when a child is lost

On a screen, it’s easy to nod along without understanding. In person, a good tutor picks up on body language, frustration, and confusion instantly, and adjusts in the moment.

Fewer distractions, better focus

Children learning at home online are surrounded by distractions. A tutor physically present creates a contained, focused environment that online sessions simply can’t replicate.

Relationships build confidence

Many children struggling academically are also struggling with confidence. The trust built through regular in-person sessions is harder to build through a screen, and often as important as the subject knowledge itself.

Frequently asked questions

Are your tutors really Oxbridge or Russell Group-educated? +

Most of our tutors are Oxbridge or Russell Group-educated, and we verify this as part of our vetting process. In exceptional circumstances, we also work with tutors who don't fit this profile but bring outstanding credentials instead, such as a long, proven track record of teaching or tutoring success, or experience as a head of department. Every tutor, regardless of background, goes through the same rigorous vetting: interview, references, and an enhanced DBS check.

What is an enhanced DBS check? +

An enhanced DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) check is the highest level of UK government background check. It confirms a person has no criminal history that would make them unsuitable to work with children. We require an enhanced DBS check for every tutor before they meet any family. It is non-negotiable.

What is your approach to safeguarding, beyond DBS checks? +

A DBS check is just the starting point. Every tutor agrees to our Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct before they’re introduced to a family, covering professional boundaries, appropriate communication with students, and clear steps for reporting any concern. For sessions involving a student under 18, a responsible adult must be present in the property throughout. We also stay involved after the introduction, not just at the start, so if anything ever feels wrong to a family or a tutor, we want to hear about it straight away.

Why use a tutoring agency rather than finding a tutor independently? +

When you find a tutor through a Facebook group or Nextdoor post, you have no way of verifying their background or knowing whether they’re the right fit for your child. A tutoring agency does all of that on your behalf: vetting every tutor, matching them carefully to your child, and remaining accountable if something isn’t working.

How do you match tutors to students? +

We don’t use an algorithm. When you contact us, we take time to understand your child: their year group, current level, target grade or school, learning style, and personality. We then identify the right tutor from our vetted pool based on subject specialism, exam board knowledge, teaching style, and location. Every match is personal.

Which subjects do you cover? +

We specialise in Maths, English and Science, from primary through to A-level, as well as music lessons. GCSE is our core focus. If you need a subject not listed, get in touch and we’ll let you know whether we can help.

What areas do you cover? +

We primarily serve Highgate, Hampstead, Muswell Hill, Crouch End and East Finchley. We also cover surrounding areas including Hornsey, Archway, Finsbury Park, Barnet, and Kentish Town. If you’re unsure whether we cover your area, get in touch and we’ll confirm.

What happens if the tutor isn’t the right fit? +

That’s exactly why we offer a discounted introductory lesson. If after the first session you feel the match isn’t right, tell us and we’ll find someone better. We stay involved throughout every placement: if something isn’t working at any point, we want to know and we’ll fix it.

Do you only offer in-person tutoring? +

Yes, we specialise exclusively in in-person tutoring. We believe a tutor sitting with your child in the same room produces better outcomes than online sessions for most students, particularly those who need confidence-building as much as content. There are plenty of online tutoring platforms if that’s what you’re looking for; we focus on doing in-person well.
