Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 2026

Kenwood Education is a private tutoring agency based in North London. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this notice. You can contact us at hello@kenwoodeducation.com or Suite 272, 17a Highgate High Street, London N6 5JT.

Our ICO registration number is ZC193627.

What Information We Collect

Depending on your relationship with us, we may collect:

  • Families — your name, contact details, your child's name, year group, school, academic needs, and any information you share with us to help us find a suitable tutor
  • Tutors — your name, contact details, qualifications, references, DBS certificate outcome, bank details, and information relating to your tutoring experience
  • Website visitors — general browsing information via Google Analytics, to help us understand how our website is used

How We Use Your Information

  • Match families with a suitable tutor
  • Vet and onboard tutors, including safeguarding checks
  • Manage bookings, invoicing, and payments
  • Communicate with you about your enquiry or ongoing arrangement
  • Meet our legal and regulatory obligations

Our Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract — to provide our matching and administrative services once you engage with us
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to enquiries, maintain our tutor roster, and run our business
  • Legal obligation — for safeguarding, tax, and other regulatory requirements
  • Consent — where we ask for it specifically (e.g. certain website tracking)

Who We Share Your Information With

We share information, where necessary, with:

  • TutorCruncher — our scheduling and invoicing platform
  • Stripe — for processing payments
  • Google Analytics — for website analytics

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Unsuccessful tutor applications: 12 months, then deleted
  • Active tutor records: 1 year from the last date we provided work-finding services to you
  • References: 1 year following a tutor's introduction to a family
  • Family/client records: 1 year from the last date we provided services to you
  • Contracts and terms of business: 6 years
  • Invoices and financial records: 6 years, in line with HMRC requirements
  • DBS certificate details: we retain only the outcome (clear/not clear) and date checked, not the certificate content itself

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, where applicable
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@kenwoodeducation.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you believe we have not handled your information properly.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use our site. This may involve cookies being placed on your device. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time. Any material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this notice or how we handle your information, please contact us at hello@kenwoodeducation.com.
