Safeguarding

Our commitment to
your family

The safety and wellbeing of the children and young people we work with matters deeply to us. Every tutor on our roster is thoroughly vetted before being introduced to a family, and we take any safeguarding concern seriously and act on it promptly.

How we vet every tutor

Before any tutor is introduced to your family, they must complete our vetting process.

✓

Structured interview

We assess their experience, approach, and suitability to work with children and young people.

✓

Two references

We obtain two references, including someone who has directly observed them teaching, tutoring, or working with children or young people.

✓

Enhanced DBS check

Every tutor completes an enhanced DBS check, registered on the Update Service where possible. We re-verify this every year, rather than treating it as a one-off check.

✓

Identity verification

We verify the tutor's identity as part of the DBS application process.

While your tutor is in your home

Safeguarding is a shared responsibility. We ask both families and tutors to follow clear expectations during tutoring sessions.

What we ask of families

  • Ensure a responsible adult is present in the property throughout any session involving a student under 18.

What we ask of tutors

  • Maintain clear, professional boundaries at all times.
  • Avoid any private or unsupervised contact with a student outside agreed sessions.
  • Never contact a student directly via personal social media or messaging outside the context of agreed tutoring.

If you ever have a concern

Please contact us directly and as soon as possible. We will listen, take it seriously, and act — whether that means a conversation with the tutor, additional support, or ending a placement entirely.

You do not need to be certain that something is wrong before speaking to us. If something concerns you, we want to hear about it.

If a child is ever in immediate danger, please call 999 first.

Questions about safeguarding?

If you'd like to know more about our safeguarding practices, please get in touch. We're happy to answer your questions.
