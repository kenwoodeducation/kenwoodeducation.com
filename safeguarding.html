Keeping children safe

Safeguarding

Kenwood Education is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of every child and young person we work with. Every tutor we work with is thoroughly vetted before they meet your family, and we take every concern seriously and act on it promptly.

How we vet every tutor

Before any tutor is introduced to your family, they will have completed every one of these checks.

Structured interview

Assessing their experience, their approach and their suitability to work with children.

Two references

Including someone who has directly observed them teaching, tutoring or working with children or young people.

Enhanced DBS check

Registered on the Update Service where possible, and re-verified every year, not just once.

Identity verification

Carried out as part of the DBS application process.

While your tutor is in your home

We ask every family to

  • Make sure a responsible adult is in the home throughout any lesson with a student under 18

We ask every tutor to

  • Keep clear, professional boundaries at all times
  • Arrange lessons with parents, not directly with students under 18
  • Avoid any private or unsupervised contact with a student outside agreed lessons
  • Never contact students through personal social media or messaging

If you ever have a concern

If a child is in immediate danger, call 999 first.

Otherwise, please contact us directly and as soon as possible. Ondi Tongchaitanawutti, Director of Kenwood Education, is our Designated Safeguarding Lead.

Call 07466 641281 WhatsApp

Or email hello@kenwoodeducation.com.

We will listen, take it seriously and act, whether that means a conversation with the tutor, extra support, or finding your family a new tutor. We keep concerns confidential, but if a child may be at risk we may need to share information with children’s services or the police.

You can also contact your local council’s children’s services directly, or call the NSPCC Helpline on 0808 800 5000 for confidential advice.
