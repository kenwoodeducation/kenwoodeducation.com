Tutor Agreement

This Agreement governs the relationship between you and Kenwood Education. By ticking the agreement checkbox on our Tutor Onboarding form and submitting your details, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Agreement.

1. About This Agreement

This Agreement governs the relationship between you (the “Tutor”) and Kenwood Education (“Kenwood,” “we,” “us”). Kenwood acts as your agent, introducing you to families seeking tuition and providing administrative support, including invoicing and payment collection. You remain a self-employed contractor throughout, responsible for your own tax and National Insurance contributions.

2. Your Status

Nothing in this Agreement creates a relationship of employment, partnership, or agency (other than Kenwood acting as your agent for introductions and billing) between you and Kenwood. You are free to offer your services to other agencies, schools, or private clients, and Kenwood is not obliged to offer you any minimum amount of work, nor are you obliged to accept any specific introduction offered.

3. Substitution

Where you are unable to deliver an agreed session yourself, you may, with the family’s agreement, arrange for another suitably qualified and vetted tutor to deliver the session in your place. Kenwood should be informed of any such arrangement.

4. Setting Your Fee

You set your own hourly rate. Kenwood adds a margin on top when invoicing the family; what you charge is what you receive. Your rate may not be changed without Kenwood’s prior written consent, and no variation to any arrangement with a family is binding unless confirmed in writing by Kenwood.

5. Invoicing and Payment

You must log each completed lesson in TutorCruncher, and submit a short lesson report, within 24 hours of the session taking place. This is what triggers invoicing, so late or incomplete logging will delay your payment. Each lesson is invoiced individually and automatically as soon as it’s marked complete. Once a family’s payment is received, funds are automatically split between Kenwood and you at the point of payment. Timing for funds reaching your account, and withdrawal frequency, are determined by TutorCruncher’s payment processing — these are platform mechanics rather than a Kenwood-set policy, and may be subject to change.

6. Expenses

Any expenses (e.g. travel, resources) must be agreed with the family in advance of being incurred. Kenwood is not responsible for reimbursing any expense that was not agreed beforehand.

7. Scheduling and Cancellations

Once introduced to a family, you are responsible for agreeing the regular session time directly with them, and for managing scheduling, rescheduling, and cancellations via TutorCruncher. If a session is cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice, you are entitled to be paid in full regardless. You may, at your discretion, waive this entitlement where a cancellation was due to genuine illness or an emergency beyond the family’s control. If you are running late for a session, it is your responsibility to arrange to make up the lost time with the family — usually at the end of the same session, or at the next lesson if that isn’t possible.

8. DBS and Right to Work

You must hold a valid enhanced DBS check, ideally registered on the Update Service, before being introduced to any family. If you do not hold one, Kenwood can apply on your behalf, at your cost; this typically takes up to two weeks. Your DBS status will be re-checked annually. You confirm that you are legally entitled to work in the UK, and agree to inform Kenwood immediately if this changes.

9. Accuracy of Information

You warrant that all information provided to Kenwood — including your qualifications, credentials, references, and suitability to provide tuition — was true when given and remains true. You agree to inform Kenwood promptly of any change to this information.

10. Conduct and Safeguarding

You agree to maintain professional standards and appropriate boundaries with students at all times, and to follow Kenwood’s Safeguarding Policy and Code of Conduct. You must report any safeguarding concern to Kenwood immediately. You may not provide any service to a family beyond the agreed tuition (e.g. mentoring, consultancy) without Kenwood’s prior written consent.

11. Data Protection

In delivering tuition, you may process personal data belonging to students and families on Kenwood’s behalf. You agree to: process this data only in line with Kenwood’s written instructions; not store family or student data on personal devices without appropriate safeguards; not transfer any such data outside the UK; notify Kenwood promptly of any data breach; and delete any such data upon request or on termination of this Agreement.

12. Confidentiality

You agree not to disclose any confidential information relating to Kenwood’s business, clients, or other tutors, both during and for 3 years following the end of this Agreement.

13. No Private Arrangements

You agree not to make any private arrangement — for tuition or payment — with any family introduced to you by Kenwood, or with any new client introduced to you by a Kenwood family, without Kenwood’s involvement. Should this occur, you agree to account to Kenwood for all sums received, without deduction, and Kenwood may seek an injunction to prevent further breaches. This restriction also applies to soliciting other Kenwood tutors. This applies for 12 months after your last session with the relevant family.

14. Referrals

If you refer a new family or a new tutor to Kenwood, and they complete 10 sessions, we’ll send you a £50 gift card as thanks.

15. Tax and Indemnity

You are responsible for your own tax and National Insurance contributions as a self-employed contractor, and agree to indemnify Kenwood against any claim, penalty, or liability arising from your tax status. You further agree to indemnify Kenwood against any claim arising from your own conduct in connection with the tuition provided.

16. Liability

Kenwood is responsible for introducing you to families and providing administrative support, but is not responsible for the content of your lessons, your teaching methods, or the academic outcomes of any student. Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or anything else which cannot be excluded by law.

17. Ending This Agreement

Either party may end this Agreement, or an individual placement, with 2 weeks’ written notice.

18. Good Practice

You are advised, though not required, to keep your own signed record of lessons delivered (or cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice), as this may assist in resolving any dispute over hours invoiced.

19. Changes to This Agreement

Kenwood may update this Agreement from time to time, with written notice of any material change.

20. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
