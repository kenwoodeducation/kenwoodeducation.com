Tutor Fee Guidance

You set your own hourly rate — this guidance is here to help, based on experience and qualifications.

At Kenwood Education, you set your own hourly rate. We add a small margin on top when we invoice the family.

We’ve put together some guidance below based on experience and qualifications. This is guidance only — you know your own experience best, and the final number is always your choice.

Suggested Rates by Experience

TierWhich tutors this suitsSuggested rate
Undergraduate You’re currently studying (2nd/3rd year or above) at university, with some tutoring experience and strong personal academic results. You may not yet hold a teaching qualification. £30–40/hr
Graduate / Experienced You’ve graduated, hold a PGCE/QTS or equivalent, or have 2–5 years of genuine 1:1 tutoring or classroom experience. £40–55/hr
Senior / Advanced You have 5+ years of sustained teaching or tutoring experience, ideally with some seniority (Head of Department, Head of Year, subject lead) or a long-standing tutoring track record with strong, evidenced outcomes. £55–70/hr
Specialist / Premium You hold exceptional, hard-to-replicate credentials — a PhD in your subject, formal examiner status, or a sustained record of placing students into top schools or Oxbridge. £70–100+/hr

A Note on Setting Your Rate

It’s worth bearing in mind that families will choose which tutor to work with partly based on rate, alongside experience and fit. Setting a rate meaningfully above what’s typical for your experience level may mean fewer families select you. Equally, undervaluing your experience isn’t necessary — families in our area are generally willing to pay well for a tutor who’s the right fit for their child.
